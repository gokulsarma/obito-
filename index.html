A Bikini Kinda Life

The layers fall away until all that’s left is our board and our favourite bikini – and that’s just the way we like it. This summer, soak up the flavor of Afro-Caribbean roots or opt for retro gingham vibes, all we need to do is have fun as we glide over glassy waves and feel the sunset glow against our skin.
With the throwback to 80s, the bikinis are high, indented and cheeky. At Billabong, we think the high-leg one piece is an essential item – easy to wear at the beach, but also as a body with your shorts. And when it’s a sporty shape, you can even use it to go surfing. From retro surf graphics and neon colours (New Wave collection), to big logos (Legacy collection), or lace and crochet details, asymmetric shoulders and irresistible prints (Main collection), the options have never been so wide.
